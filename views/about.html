About

Every calendar day has a past. This site lets you turn the dial and read it.

How to use it

  • Drag the outer ring of the dial to change the day, the middle disc to change the month, or the blue centre to scrub through the whole year.
  • Prefer controls? Use the month and day menus, the arrows, or Today. On a keyboard, ← and → step one day, and / jumps to search.
  • Switch between events, births, deaths and holidays, search within the day, and flip the order from oldest to newest.
  • The address always holds your date and category, so you can copy it and share exactly what you are looking at.

Where the history comes from

All historical entries come live from Wikipedia's On This Day feed. Nothing is written or edited here. Text is available under CC BY-SA 4.0, and every entry links back to the Wikipedia article it came from. The server keeps a copy of each day for a few hours so the page loads fast and Wikipedia is not asked the same question twice.

The visitor wall and your privacy

If you post on the Visitors page, we store exactly what you type: your name, the place you entered (optional), the date you picked (optional), your note, and the time you posted it. All of that is shown publicly.

  • We do not store your IP address, email or any account. There are no cookies and no analytics.
  • To limit spam, the server counts posts per IP address in temporary memory. That counter is not written to the database.
  • Please do not post anything you would not want public. Notes with links are rejected.
  • To have a note removed, contact the site owner through GitHub and mention the name and text of the note.
